# Evidence repository maintenance

- Present the complete evidence and its methods directly. README files and method descriptions explain the current package rather than the writing or review process.
- Keep statements concise and proportionate to their evidence. State material scope and uncertainty once, where they affect interpretation.
- Omit writing-tool declarations, review-round narratives, internal tasks and statements about satisfying reviewers. Use the recorded source, version, passage and operation to explain an assignment.
- Integrate changes into the relevant documentation and records. Keep counts, links, bibliographies and generated tables consistent; figure, table and citation numbers follow their first appearance in the manuscript.
- Preserve original source and retrieval records. Historical versions remain accessible through Git tags; do not rewrite published tags or convert missing records into verified findings.
- Validate package hashes and record relationships before publication. Publish research annotations and permitted evidence assets; keep third-party full texts, private manuscripts and credentials outside this repository.
